What you're agreeing to
  • You own or manage these animals, and the information on your form is accurate.
  • MSU sends the TSUs to its genotyping lab and reports genetic conditions and parentage back to you.
  • You'll be invoiced for the TSUs we receive.
  • You retain ownership of your animals' genotypes.
  • Results depend on sample quality and the information you provide. Some samples may fail and need resampling, and results are reported as-is without guarantee.
  • Your contact information and individual flock results will not be shared publicly.

Before you upload

  • Every animal needs its TSU barcode and an EID and/or flock tag.
  • Keep EIDs formatted as text so all 15 digits survive. If Excel shows 8.40003E+14, format the column as Text and re-enter.
  • Not sure of an older ewe's birth year? Enter a range like 2018-2020.
  • For parentage, genotypes are needed on sire and dam. Please send samples or tell us which were tested before to test against.
  • Delete the EXAMPLE row before you upload.
