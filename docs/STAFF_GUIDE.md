# Staff guide: receiving boxes and building batches

You only need two web pages. You don't need the Google Sheet.

- **Receiving:** https://cjposbergh.github.io/MSUSheepGenotyping/staff/receive.html
- **Batches:** https://cjposbergh.github.io/MSUSheepGenotyping/staff/batches.html

Bookmark both on the bench computer (and your phone, if you scan with it).

## Signing in
Type the staff passphrase (ask Christian; never write it down anywhere public) and **your own name**. Your name is recorded on every box you receive and every batch you make. The computer remembers you until someone presses **Lock** (top right). Press Lock when you leave a shared computer. If the page sends you back to sign-in, the passphrase has changed: ask Christian.

## Receiving a box
1. **Open the box on the page.** Scan the QR code on the packing slip, or type the reference printed on it (like `P004-01`, or `P003-01-R1` for replacements). No slip? Scan any TSU in the box: the page finds the submission it belongs to.
2. **Look for the consent form** if the yellow box says so. If a signed paper consent form is in the box, tick **Signed paper consent form is in the box**, choose the research answer (Yes/No) exactly as ticked on the form, and type the name it's signed by. Photograph the form for the Drive folder, then file the paper. No form? Carry on: the samples stay on hold until the producer signs, and they get reminders automatically.
3. **Scan every TSU.** The cursor sits in the scan box, so a USB/Bluetooth scanner just works: scan, scan, scan. On a phone with Chrome, **Scan with camera** keeps reading barcodes until you press Done. You can also type a barcode and press Enter.
   - Blue **received**: ticked off.
   - Amber **scanned twice**: nothing changed, carry on.
   - Red **not on this submission**: it belongs to another box (the page says which) or to nothing. **Set it aside** and tell Christian.
4. **Finish receiving.** Check the summary: how many were received, which are missing (they'll be marked Not received and the producer is told). Press **Mark received and write the email**.
5. **The email.** Read the draft to the producer, fix anything, and press **Send email and finish**. (Only use "Finish without emailing" if Christian asked you to.)
6. **Receive another box**, or **Reopen** if a TSU turns up after you finished (scan it, finish again, and send the updated email).

## Building a batch for GenomNZ
1. Open **Batches**. **Ready to ship** lists every submission whose samples are received, signed and not yet in a batch. Grey rows are on hold (not signed) and can't be picked.
2. Untick anything not going in this box. The panel shows how many TSUs are selected.
3. **Create batch and download GenomNZ file.** The file (like `B2026-03_GenomNZ.xlsx`) downloads and a copy is saved to Drive automatically. If the page says something needs fixing first (a missing breed or birth year), tell Christian.
4. Send the file to GenomNZ with the box, as Christian has asked.
5. When the box actually goes out, set the **Ship date** and press **Mark as shipped**. Producers get an email that their samples are at the lab.
   - Changed your mind before shipping? **Cancel this batch** puts the samples back in Ready to ship. Throw away the file you downloaded.
   - Came back later? A batch not yet shipped shows **Open** under All batches. Any batch's file can be downloaded again from there.

## Good to know
- Never type into the Google Sheet (you don't have access, and you don't need it).
- Replacement TSUs arrive in their own small bag with a **replacement slip** (reference ending in `-R1`, `-R2`…). Receive them exactly like a box.
- Something odd (a box with no slip and no TSUs we know, a torn tube, a producer at the door)? Set it aside, note the reference if there is one, and email christian.posbergh@montana.edu.
